# V32 Corporate Domain UX Contract

Scope: Employees, Employee Drawer, Licenses, Content Center.

- No database/migration/auth/API contract changes.
- Page headers use a single title/description stack.
- Metric grids are responsive: 4 / 2 / 1 columns.
- Data rows use explicit grid columns and wrap long values.
- Actions may wrap; they must not overflow the viewport.
- Drawer content scrolls independently and remains keyboard reachable.
- Form labels/descriptions are separate blocks.
- Empty/error states have a minimum presentation surface.
- Reduced motion is respected.
